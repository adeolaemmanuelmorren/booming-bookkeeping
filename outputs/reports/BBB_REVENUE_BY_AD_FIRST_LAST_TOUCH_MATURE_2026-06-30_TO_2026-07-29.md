# BBB revenue by ad: first and last paid touch

Paid-click cohort window: **June 30–July 29, 2026**, using `America/Los_Angeles` dates.

July 29 is 14 days before the August 12 report date. The report assigns all subsequently observed BBB revenue back to the qualifying paid click and its ad.

- **First paid touch:** earliest observed paid click before the KRC registration.
- **Last paid touch:** latest observed paid click before the KRC registration.
- **BBB:** first successful, non-repeat payment over $900 per profile.

The first-touch and last-touch totals differ because the 30-day window is applied to the attributed click. For example, a registrant can have a first paid click before June 30 and a last paid click inside this window.
